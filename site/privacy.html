Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Work-Love-Balance is a Chrome extension that shows your bookmarks on the new tab page. In short: it does not collect, sell or send your data anywhere.

What the extension accesses

Bookmarks — to display, search, rename, move, archive and delete them at your request. All of this happens locally in your browser.

Storage — to remember your settings, favorites, column order and personal quotes. Stored locally by Chrome (theme may sync via your own Chrome account).

Favicons — to show site icons, using Chrome's built-in favicon cache.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no ads. Your bookmarks never leave your browser.

Network requests

The extension does not send any of your data to any server. Site icons are served by Chrome itself.
